BorderMark Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026
App: BorderMark (Android / iOS)
Android package: com.bordermark.app

BorderMark helps you record stays, visas, documents, and travel rules on your own device. This policy explains what information the app handles and your choices.

Summary

  • BorderMark is local-first: your data is stored on your device.
  • There is no BorderMark account, no cloud sync, and no analytics or ad trackers in the app.
  • Data leaves your device only when you choose (for example encrypted backup or the system share sheet).

Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use BorderMark, the app may store:

  • Profile details (name, birth date, citizenships, optional profile photo)
  • Travel stays, documents, attachments, rules, reminders, and notes
  • App settings and optional app lock PIN (secure device storage)

We do not operate a server that receives this content by default.

Permissions

Permission Use
Location (while in use) Only when you choose “Use current location” for onboarding or stays. No background tracking.
Camera / Photos Only when you attach photos or files you select.
Notifications Local reminders on your device; not sent through BorderMark servers.
Biometrics Only if you enable app lock with Face ID, fingerprint, or similar.

Backup and sharing

You may create encrypted .bm backup files with a password you choose. Share and export use your device’s share sheet; third-party apps you pick are governed by their own policies.

What we do not do

  • Sell your personal information
  • Use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app
  • Require login to use the app
  • Automatically upload location or documents to BorderMark

App stores and builds

App stores may collect download and device information under their policies. Release builds are compiled with standard developer tools; that process does not include your personal BorderMark data.

Deletion

Delete data in the app, remove backup files you saved elsewhere, or uninstall BorderMark to remove local app data from your device.

Children

BorderMark is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy and change the effective date above.
