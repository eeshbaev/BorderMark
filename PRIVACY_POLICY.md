# BorderMark Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 27, 2026  
**App:** BorderMark (Android / iOS)  
**Package name (Android):** `com.bordermark.app`

BorderMark helps you record stays, visas, documents, and travel rules on your own device. This policy explains what information the app handles and your choices.

## Summary

- BorderMark is **local-first**: your stays, documents, and settings are stored on your device in a private database.
- There is **no BorderMark account**, **no cloud sync**, and **no analytics or advertising trackers** built into the app.
- Data leaves your device **only when you choose** (for example encrypted backup export or the system share sheet).

## Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use BorderMark, the app may store:

- Profile details you enter (such as name, birth date, citizenships, and optional profile photo)
- Travel stays (countries, cities, dates, stay type, visa notes)
- Documents (passports, visas, permits, expiry dates, optional document numbers and notes)
- Attachments you add (photos or PDFs linked to stays or documents)
- Rules, reminders, and notes you create
- App settings (appearance, default country, date format, notification preferences)
- Optional app lock PIN (stored in the device’s secure storage, not in the main database)

We do not operate a server that receives this content by default.

## Permissions and optional features

BorderMark may request device permissions **only when a feature needs them**:

| Permission | Why it is used |
|------------|----------------|
| **Location (while in use)** | Only if you tap an action such as “Use current location” to suggest a country during onboarding or when adding a stay. We do not track your location in the background. |
| **Camera** | Only when you choose to take a photo for a stay or document attachment. |
| **Photo library** | Only when you choose to pick an image or file from your library. |
| **Notifications** | To show local reminders you configure (document expiry, rules, reminders). Notifications are scheduled on your device; they are not sent through BorderMark servers. |
| **Biometrics / device authentication** | Only if you enable app lock and choose to unlock with Face ID, fingerprint, or similar. |

You can deny permissions and still use most of the app by entering information manually.

## Encrypted backup and sharing

- **Backup:** You may create an encrypted `.bm` backup file protected by a password you choose. The file is written to storage you select; BorderMark does not upload it automatically.
- **Share / export:** If you use share or export features, your device’s share sheet sends data to apps **you** select. We do not control those third-party services.

## What we do not do

- Sell your personal information
- Use third-party analytics or crash reporting SDKs in the app for advertising
- Require an email address or social login to use the app
- Automatically upload your location, documents, or stay history to BorderMark

## App distribution and developer tools

BorderMark is distributed through app stores (such as Google Play). Store providers may collect information about downloads and device compatibility according to their own policies.

When we build the app for release, we use standard developer tooling (for example Expo Application Services) to compile the application binary. **That process does not include your personal BorderMark data**, which remains on your device unless you export it yourself.

## Data retention and deletion

- Data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, restore from backup, or uninstall BorderMark.
- Uninstalling the app removes local app data from the device subject to your operating system’s behavior.
- You are responsible for copies of backup files you save elsewhere.

## Children’s privacy

BorderMark is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy at the same public URL and update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
